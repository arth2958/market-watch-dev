Entry rules and limits

Only five input fields: ticker, acquired, quantity, entry price and side. The page assigns row numbers automatically. Acquired is the date bought/opened (YYYY-MM-DD), not a holding-period tax conclusion. Use split-adjusted quantity and basis for the same current share units. Fees and basis adjustments should already be reflected in entry price.

Each row is one lot. Distinct lots in the same ticker are allowed; exact duplicate rows are rejected because account identity is not collected. Use Edit row to change any of the five fields, then Save changes or Cancel edit. You can also remove rows. Maximum 5,000 lots / 2 MB pasted text. Invalid or unpriced pasted rows reject the entire batch; existing rows remain unchanged. No guess or zero price is substituted.

Automatic price lookup uses the bundled public-universe closing marks. No entered ticker or quantity is sent to a quote service. A ticker outside the dataset is shown as unavailable and is not added. Closing prices may be stale, and are not live or executable quotes. Reload loads the currently published bundle but clears entries. No scheduled market-data refresh is active in this preview.

Stocks and ETFs only. No option contracts, option Greeks, automatic account-type inference, transaction history or cross-account wash-sale check. Tax status and wash-sale/straddle clearance are always unverified.

Correlation method and coverage

Positive Pearson correlation of matched daily adjusted-close simple returns. Rank by the latest 252 market sessions, minimum 200; 2-year comparison uses the latest 504 market sessions, minimum 400. Dates and sample size are shown for each pair. No forward filling. A highest value means highest measured within this stated universe, not every security in existence. A stock/ETF outside the universe has no replacement result. Prices are delayed closing snapshots, not executable quotes. ETF holdings overlap and substantially-identical status are not determined by correlation. Same-issuer share classes are excluded when known.

High correlation can change. The best statistical match may have different leverage, valuation, business exposure, liquidity or tax treatment. For a short, the candidate is a same-direction research comparison only; borrow availability, fees and dividend obligations are not checked. Option P/L is not replicated by underlying-price correlation.

Before a loss becomes a tax harvest
  • Confirm taxable ownership, exact lot basis and share identification with your broker. Retirement-account losses are not normal taxable capital losses.
  • Review purchases, DRIPs, options and planned buys during the 30 days before and after a loss sale. Include other accounts and spouse activity as applicable. An IRA/Roth repurchase can disallow a loss without the usual basis increase.
  • "Highly correlated" is not a substantially-identical safe harbor. Different ticker, issuer or a 31-day wait alone is not a complete tax determination.
  • Short-sale completion, holding-period and wash-sale rules differ. Offsetting positions and options can invoke straddle loss deferral or constructive-sale rules. Trader status is not a blanket exemption.
  • Estimated loss is not estimated tax savings. Review realized gains, costs, exposure changes and your tax situation with your tax adviser.

Primary references: IRS Publication 550 (2025), Schedule D instructions (2025), Revenue Ruling 2008-5. Latest available guidance checked November 15, 2026. No tax clearance is supplied.
