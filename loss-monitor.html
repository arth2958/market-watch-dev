Correlation availability

High correlation is not a substantially-identical safe harbor or tax clearance. Statistical replacement research is paused, not replaced by an unmeasured guess.

Before a loss becomes a tax harvest
  • Confirm taxable ownership, exact lot basis and share identification with your broker. Retirement-account losses are not normal taxable capital losses.
  • Review purchases, DRIPs, options and planned buys during the 30 days before and after a loss sale. Include other accounts and spouse activity as applicable. An IRA/Roth repurchase can disallow a loss without the usual basis increase.
  • "Highly correlated" is not a substantially-identical safe harbor. Different ticker, issuer or a 31-day wait alone is not a complete tax determination.
  • Short-sale completion, holding-period and wash-sale rules differ. Offsetting positions and options can invoke straddle loss deferral or constructive-sale rules. Trader status is not a blanket exemption.
  • Estimated loss is not estimated tax savings. Review realized gains, costs, exposure changes and your tax situation with your tax adviser.
